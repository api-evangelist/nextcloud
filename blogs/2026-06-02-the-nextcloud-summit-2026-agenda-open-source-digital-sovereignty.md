The Nextcloud Summit 2026 takes place on June 9 in Munich, featuring keynotes from industry leaders and the launch of Nextcloud Hub 26 Spring. The full-day conference addresses digital sovereignty and open-source collaboration through parallel tracks for enterprises, public institutions, and technical professionals, along with panel discussions and networking opportunities. Key sessions focus on Europe's technology landscape and strategies for reducing dependency on Big Tech platforms.
